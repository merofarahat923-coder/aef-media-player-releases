Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: AEF Media Player collects no data about you and has no ads or tracking. It only goes online for things you ask for, and this page lists exactly where and why.

What stays on your computer

  • Settings: language and look, recent files, where you stopped each video, bookmarks, favourites, library folders, reminders, and the channels and films you watched from IPTV accounts (for "Continue watching"), in %APPDATA%\AEF Media Player.
  • IPTV accounts: the password is stored encrypted for your Windows account and never written in readable form to any file or log.
  • Temporary files: channel logos and library pictures in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AEF Media Player, and a small log (aef.log, beside the program or in %APPDATA%\AEF Media Player) to help solve problems, with no passwords in it.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

When the player goes online

Phone remote (only when you open it)Your phone, on your home networkThe player opens an address on your home network only, locked by a random secret code. The phone sees what is playing and the channel list; nobody outside your home can reach it.
Play on TVThe TV on your home networkOnly the video you chose, on your local network, at a random temporary address closed when you stop.

Problem reports

After a problem the player may offer to send a report. It is never sent by itself: it opens in your browser for you to read and decide. It holds the version, the Windows version and the last lines of the log, with your name, file names and any passwords removed. Reports are posted as GitHub issues, which are public, so please do not add anything personal.

What we do not do

  • No usage statistics, no tracking of what you watch.
  • No ads or ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
